Hardware:
Used the TL082 op-amp for testing. Specifically wired like so:

V+: 9V
V-: -9V
Inverting input -> Output (feedback)
Non-Inverting input -> Signal out of R-2R.

I then added 2 Low Pass Filters using 470Ω resistors and 220pF capacitors and buffered it again using the second amp in the TL082.

Now I have "usable" signal.

Frequency is acceptable up to 100-200Khz (acceptable meaning no obvious differences from my Generator's 50Khz).
Can output up to 1MHz, where the signal has 50% gain and ~1V dc offset; A lot of jitter and ghosting.
