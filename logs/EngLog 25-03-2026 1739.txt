Progress. Completed first 1MHz prototype. Signal is far from clean and there appears to be a "duty cycle variety". 
It's very clear that the signal looks very unstable and noisy. Yet, it is a 1MHz square signal on a breadboard.

Used a totem pole circuit (available in hardware schematics in LTSpice). 

Had many issues regarding higher frequencies, ended up adding a pnp (and thus making the totem pole circuit) to "pull" down the signal in time. 
Right after there were issues pulling "up" the signal, but those were remedied (to a degree) by a 30pF capacitor before the connected base of the first pair of transistors. I added it in hopes it would give more "power" to the signal allowing it to hit Vmax faster.

Current ability: 1kOhm input impendance. More than that, the transistors and resistors get warm and the circuit experiences a big voltage drop. Although the factors are known and are 2:
- High Collector resistance (relative to the 50Ohm output needed)
- Battery can't drive that much current.

Big milestone today. Even if 1Mhz is noisy, up to 500kHz the signal looks pretty good.
