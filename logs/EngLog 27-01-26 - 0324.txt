First official log.

Today I cleared everything. Started from scratch.

Installed pico workspace through the official site as well as drivers using zadig. Changes and firmware updates became much easier.

Carried on work and made a very inefficient 10k,20k R-2R ladder.

For time and prototyping reasons used Gemini to generate test code for the ladder. As expected, visible quality loss starts happening at 60-70kHz. Can still produce okay signals up to 200kHz. Seems the filter cuts off about there as quality severely decreases for higher frequencies, so does the magnitude.

Pushed to it's limits the pico can provide ~10MHz signal.

Next steps:
	- Changing the ladder to use 1k and 2k resistors in order to help raise the cutoff frequency.
	- Learn to program the Pico with the short term goal of writing a frequency in the serial monitor and have the circuit provide the signal in that frequency with error <0.001%
